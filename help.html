Frequently Asked Questions

How do I submit solutions?

Choose a problem from the Problemset tab, select your preferred programming language (C++, Java, Python, JavaScript), and upload your code file or paste it in the online IDE.

What are the contest rules?

Contests run for a fixed duration (usually 2 hours). Solutions are judged automatically against pre-configured test cases.

How does time penalty work?

Penalty is calculated from the start of the contest plus 10–20 minutes for each rejected submission attempt prior to solving.

Need Support?

Encountered a bug or issue? Reach out to the site admins directly.
